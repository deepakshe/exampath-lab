The Expenditure Law: When price increases by +a/b, consumption must decrease by -a/(a+b) to keep total expenditure constant. (Example: Price rises 25% [+1/4] → Consumption must fall by 1/(1+4) = 1/5 = 20%).

DAY 3: Reasoning Inequality Hierarchy & Either-Or Checklist

The 3-Tier Priority System

Along any continuous chain without opposite signs:

  • Rank 1: The King (>, <) — If present at least once, King MUST be in the conclusion.
  • Rank 2: The Soldier (≥, ≤) — Second priority when King is absent.
  • Rank 3: The Citizen (=) — Neutral bridge connecting elements.

Opposite Signs Barrier: When signs face in opposite directions (e.g. A > B < C), the relationship between A and C is INDETERMINATE.

Either-Or Condition: Both elements same + Both individually false + All 3 signs (> , < , =) covered.
